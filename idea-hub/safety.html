Reporting & Safety

Idea Hub is moderated, but it is not monitored continuously and cannot guarantee a response time.

Report a discussion or reply

Sign in with a verified email address, open the discussion, and select Report or Report reply. Explain the issue without repeating unnecessary personal information. Your report goes to the private moderator queue. Moderators can remove the content and administrators can suspend accounts.

Account support, privacy, and appeals

Choose Account support in the footer to request account deletion, correction of information, assistance, or an appeal. Suspended members can use this form. If you cannot sign in, try Forgot password first.

Fallback: open an issue in the Idea Hub repository asking for a private contact method. GitHub issues are public: do not include your email, password, identifiers, private information, or details of a sensitive report. A GitHub account is required for that fallback.

Immediate danger

Contact local emergency services if someone is in immediate danger. Do not rely on this community or its reporting form for emergency help.

Protect yourself

Never share passwords, authentication links, account recovery codes, precise location, financial details, or other sensitive information. Display names are not proof of identity. Be cautious of requests for money and off-site links. Verify instructions before starting electrical, mechanical, chemical, or other potentially dangerous projects.

Security concerns

Do not post exploit details or credentials publicly. Use Account support to request a private security contact, or the public fallback only to request that contact. Describe the affected page without including secrets.
