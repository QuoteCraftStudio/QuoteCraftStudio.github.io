Community Guidelines

Effective: October 14, 2026

Idea Hub is intended for constructive discussion of practical ideas, projects, technology, and questions.

Be respectful

Do not harass, threaten, bully, impersonate, or target other people. Disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.

Keep it safe

Do not post illegal content, instructions intended to harm people, malware, scams, spam, or content that exploits or endangers others.

Protect privacy

Do not publish passwords, financial information, private addresses, identification numbers, or another person's private information without permission.

Post responsibly

Use clear titles, stay reasonably on topic, and do not repeatedly post the same material or misleading promotions. Only share material you have the right to share.

Moderation

Content may be removed or restricted when it violates these guidelines. Repeated or serious violations may lead to account restrictions once member accounts are enabled.

These guidelines may be updated as Idea Hub develops.
