Community Guidelines

Effective October 16, 2026. Help make Idea Hub a useful place to learn and build together.

Choose a space

  • Ideas & Inventions: useful concepts, improvements, and thoughtful feedback.
  • Projects & DIY: making, repairing, experimenting, and sharing progress.
  • Technology & Apps: software, devices, digital tools, and practical techniques.
  • Questions & Help: clear questions and patient, helpful answers.

Make your contribution useful

Give your discussion a descriptive title, enough context, and a specific question or idea. Credit sources and distinguish facts from opinions. Explain risks when sharing projects. Disagree with ideas respectfully and welcome beginners.

What is not allowed

No profanity, personal insults, bullying, harassment, threats, hate, sexual exploitation, illegal content, impersonation, scams, spam, malicious code, or attempts to bypass account or moderation controls. Do not publish other people's personal information, private messages, copyrighted content without permission, or confidential ideas.

Automated screening

Submitted public text, including profiles and edits, is screened using Cloudflare Workers AI and basic language/spam rules. Clearly prohibited submissions are rejected with a message. If screening is unavailable or inconclusive, changes are not saved; try again later or contact Account support. No account is automatically suspended by AI. Screening is imperfect and does not replace human judgment.

Moderation

Posts and edits are reviewed before appearing publicly. Moderators may approve or remove content, lock or pin discussions, and review reports. Administrators can suspend accounts and appoint moderators. Approval does not mean the content is endorsed or verified.

Help us improve the community

Use Report on a discussion or reply when something violates these rules. Use Account support for an appeal or privacy concern. Do not retaliate or repeat harmful content in another public post.
