Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026. This policy applies to Idea Hub at quotecraftstudio.github.io/idea-hub/. QuoteCraft Studio operates the community.

What is public

Your display name, bio, profile join date, approved discussions and replies, and content timestamps are public. Anyone may read, copy, or share public content. Search engines may discover it. Avoid real names or personal details you do not want published.

What we collect and why

Firebase Authentication handles your email address, password authentication, verification, and password reset. Your sign-in details pass over HTTPS through our Cloudflare Worker to Firebase. We do not store your password in the discussion database.

The community database stores your authentication identifier, a separate public profile identifier, display name, bio, posts, replies, timestamps, policy version associated with contributions, moderation role, and suspension state. These support accounts, ownership checks, discussions, and moderation. The agreement is accepted at registration and on each sign-in rather than on every post or comment. The service returns a short-lived, cryptographically signed proof of that acceptance tied to your signed-in session. The proof is kept in your browser tab's memory, not persistent browser storage; discussions and replies still record their associated agreement version.

Private reports and account support requests are visible to moderators. Do not include passwords or sensitive personal information. Moderation actions and the acting account identifier are recorded for accountability. Administrators can use the Firebase dashboard to manage authentication accounts.

Providers and technical information

GitHub Pages hosts public site files. Cloudflare hosts the API and D1 database. Google Firebase provides authentication and transactional email. These providers process connection information, including IP addresses, under their own policies, and may process data outside your country. Our application uses a secret-based hash of your IP address and account identifier for short-lived rate limits; it does not store raw IP addresses in its database.

Authentication tokens stay in this tab's memory and are cleared when you reload or sign out. The application adds no advertising, tracking analytics, cookies, or local-storage tracking. Providers may use their own operational and security systems.

Automated screening

Cloudflare Workers AI screens submitted display names, biographies, discussion titles, messages, and edits for prohibited content. Only that submitted public text is sent for screening; our application does not include account emails, passwords, authentication tokens, or private reports in AI requests. Avoid including private information in public text. Automated decisions can be mistaken; use Account support to request review. Posts, comments, and edits cleared by automated screening become public without advance human approval. Flagged submissions are kept private for moderator review. Automated screening is imperfect; report content that should be removed.

Retention and control

Profiles and contributions remain while the account and community need them. Deleting a post removes its message text from the active database and public view; titles, attribution, status, timestamps, and related records may remain. Copies in provider backups, caches, or copies held by other people may persist. Rate-limit records expire by hour and are cleaned up daily when the maintenance schedule is enabled.

Reports, moderation records, and account information may remain until reviewed for removal, including where needed to investigate abuse or meet legal obligations. Use Account support to request access, correction, account deletion, or removal of identifying information. Requests require identity verification. Do not submit new content if you do not want it processed as described.

Privacy questions

Use Account support after signing in. Suspended accounts can still submit support requests. If sign-in fails, use the fallback route on the safety page and ask for a private contact method without including personal data publicly. This is a community for adults; we do not knowingly seek registrations from children.
