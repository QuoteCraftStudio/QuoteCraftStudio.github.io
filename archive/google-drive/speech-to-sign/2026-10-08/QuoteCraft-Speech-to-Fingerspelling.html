Speech to fingerspelling

First-stage prototype: ASL alphabet. This spells English words one letter at a time. It does not translate sentences into ASL or show a full signing person.

English A–Z only. Numbers and other symbols are skipped with a notice. J and Z need movement; a static picture is not a complete demonstration. Repeated letters are separated by a brief visual gap. Images load from Wikimedia Commons; if an image fails, the app shows a notice instead of inventing a hand shape. Speech recognition availability depends on the browser; HTTPS is usually required. Speech may be processed by your browser’s speech provider. Text is not saved by this app.Illustration source: wpclipart ASL alphabet on Wikimedia Commons. This prototype has not been reviewed by a fluent ASL signer.
