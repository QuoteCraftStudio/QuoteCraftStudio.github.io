Find the APK in Downloads and open it. If Android asks, allow installation from the browser or Files app you’re using.

03

Install & open

Tap Install, then Open. Speech apps may ask for microphone access. Choose “While using the app” if offered.

These are direct downloads, not Google Play listings. If you have an older AppsGeyser version, export saved data before uninstalling it. The clean builds use different app packages and do not automatically transfer your old data.

A few things to know

These are early versions. Build and signature checks passed, but full testing on Android devices is still in progress. Features, voices and speech recognition can vary by phone. No AppsGeyser or advertising SDKs are included in these APK builds.

Speech may be processed by your Android speech provider. Several apps open hosted websites and need an internet connection. Any accounts or services used by those websites have their own privacy terms.

This download page does not ask you to create an account. Installation and microphone permission remain under your control.
