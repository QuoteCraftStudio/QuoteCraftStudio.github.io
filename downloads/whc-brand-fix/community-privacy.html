IdeaHub privacy notice

Version 1.2 · Released October 13, 2026

This notice covers the IdeaHub community and its moderation tools. Contact the publisher about privacy, access, correction, or deletion at quotecraft300@gmail.com.

Accounts and sign-in

When account connections are enabled, Firebase Authentication processes your email, password, account identifier, verification state, and sign-in activity. Firebase sends verification and password reset emails when you request them. Your password is sent directly to Firebase and is not stored in the forum database. This website uses Firebase session persistence for the current browser tab; signing out clears its local sign-in session. Provider records may remain under Firebase's retention practices.

The forum server verifies your Firebase identity token and verified email before accepting a submission. A publisher-managed list of account identifiers controls moderator access. No shared moderator password or secret is included in the public website files.

What is public and what stays private

An approved post shows its category, title or reply text, chosen public nickname, post number, and date. Anyone can read or copy public content; search engines and other services may retain copies. Use a nickname and omit sensitive details.

Submitted content, its moderation status, your account identifier, verified email, and agreement version are stored in the connected Cloudflare D1 database. Your email and account identifier are not included in public forum responses. Authorized moderators can review private submissions and contact details. These details support account verification, moderation, abuse prevention, and handling requests; posting does not subscribe you to a mailing list.

Network and service data

Cloudflare handles requests to the forum. The service uses a salted hash derived from the apparent network address for short-term abuse controls. The application does not store the raw address in its forum tables; hosting and authentication providers may separately process network addresses, device information, or operational logs.

Firebase browser modules are loaded from Google's content delivery service when sign-in is configured. Account data is handled by Google/Firebase; forum data is handled by Cloudflare. Processing locations and provider retention depend on the publisher's selected services and provider settings. Read Firebase privacy information and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Retention, removal, and backups

When the configured cleanup schedule runs, application abuse hashes older than two days and post contact emails older than 90 days are cleared. These are cleanup thresholds, not promises of removal at an exact hour. Accounts in Firebase, account identifiers on posts, and post content are handled separately.

Posts and replies are not automatically deleted because of their age. Publisher removal hides a record and marks it for recoverable moderation. An authorized moderator may restore it. Owner-controlled backups and provider backups or logs may retain earlier copies, and public copies cannot always be recalled. Deleting a Firebase account does not automatically remove its forum posts. Request content removal or permanent deletion by contacting QuoteCraft; the publisher must review and carry out the request across the applicable systems.

Home screen, offline help, and optional donations

IdeaHub can be added to the home screen when your browser supports it. The app may store a separate public offline help page, rules, privacy notice, disclaimer, styles and QC logo in the browser cache. The service worker does not cache discussion responses, the signed-in forum page, moderator page, runtime account settings, sign-in tokens, or authenticated API responses. Firebase session storage is separate from this public offline cache.

Optional donations use the existing PayPal support link and open PayPal in a separate tab. That service handles payment details under its own terms and privacy practices. IdeaHub does not collect card details or run a payment form, and a donation does not create an app account or subscribe you to a mailing list.

Your choices

You can read public discussions without creating an account. Posting is optional. Before each submission, read the rules and this notice and tick the agreement box. Do not submit personal information belonging to children or other people. Sign out when using a shared device.

The separate email link opens your own email application. Nothing is sent until you press Send there. Email is handled by your mail service and the publisher's mail service, outside the forum database and its cleanup schedule.

The current source uses the publisher's public Firebase web settings and forum service address. Public posting remains controlled by the server and is disabled until setup and verification are complete. Publishing these files alone does not confirm that the connected service is ready.
