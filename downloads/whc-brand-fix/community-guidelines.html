Community rules

Version 1.1 · Updated October 12, 2026

Share with care

Keep discussions constructive and relevant to apps, games, puzzles, learning, installation, creative projects, and feedback. No harassment, threats, hate speech, scams, illegal material, advertising spam, sexually explicit content, or disclosure of someone else's private information.

Do not submit passwords, payment details, identity documents, home addresses, or personal information about children. This is a place for creative ideas and general help. Posts do not provide professional financial, legal, or medical advice.

Verified accounts and moderation

You need an email and password account with a verified email address to submit topics or replies. Choose a public nickname that does not identify you unnecessarily. Your email is excluded from public forum responses.

Every new post waits for publisher approval. QuoteCraft may reject or remove content. Removal hides content from public discussions and keeps a recoverable record for authorized moderation; it does not promise immediate permanent erasure. Contact QuoteCraft about privacy or deletion requests.

Your contributions

You retain ownership of your submitted content. By submitting it, you give QuoteCraft permission to store and moderate it, display approved content publicly, and consider the ideas for future products. Submitting an idea does not guarantee publication, a response, payment, or development of an app.

Respect other people's rights and only submit content you have permission to share. Ordinary participation and personal app data remain available; only the publisher or someone directly authorized by the publisher may issue official QuoteCraft software updates. Existing software licences and mandatory legal rights continue to apply.

Before you submit

Read the forum privacy notice v1.2. Each post and reply has an unchecked agreement box. The service records the acknowledgement version with the submission.
